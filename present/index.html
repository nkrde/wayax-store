The mental model

Nobody reads their phone battery in mAh.

Phone

4,500 mAh · 3.85 V · screen, GPS, 5G drain

62%

WayaX AI

48,213 tokens · 4 models · 412.6 credits

62%

Hidden: the engine

Tokens, models and credits still get tracked, just out of sight.

Shown: one number

“How much is left?” is answered in a glance. No maths needed.

Everyone already knows how to read a battery. Nobody thinks in milliamp-hours or voltage; they just glance at the percentage and decide whether to charge. AI usage works the same way. Behind the scenes we still meter tokens, models and credits. On screen the user sees one percentage of their plan.

How it works

Complex in the engine, simple on screen

1 · METER

Tokens, models and tool calls get tracked

2 · WEIGH

Each answer gets weighed by its type: 1 · 2 · 3 · 10

3 · SHOW

62% used

0–80% · Just use it

No interruptions and no counting

80% · Nudge

One quiet banner

100%

Top up, or wait for the reset

Step 1: the backend keeps metering everything (tokens, model costs, tool calls), which protects margins. Step 2: each answer gets weighed by its type, so a quick answer counts 1 and a deep research report counts 10. Step 3: the user sees one percentage. Clear thresholds sit on that bar: below 80% nothing interrupts the user, at 80% one quiet nudge appears, and at 100% they top up or wait for the reset. Switching or adding models never changes what the user sees.

Weekly vs monthly · 1

Same total usage. Very different waits.

150 units a week ≈ 650 a month. Here’s a heavy user who burns through it in results week.

Weekly limit · resets every Monday

The total allowance is the same either way: about 150 units a week or 650 a month. The difference is how long someone waits after running out. On a weekly limit, the worst case is a few days and usually it’s two or three. On a monthly limit, a heavy week early in the month can lock someone out for three weeks.

Weekly vs monthly · 3

Recommendation: use each where it fits

PRO

Weekly

Frequent, heavy users. Short waits keep them asking, and topping up.

FREE ACCESS

Daily

New users try it: 3 questions a day for the first 3 days, then AI and calls lock.

TOP-UPS

Carry over

Carried past the Monday reset and valid 3 months, so buying one never feels wasted.

If Pro moves to monthly anyway, change three things:

1 · Top-up sizes: define them as a share of the month, not the week.

2 · Price the ₹99 pack: against a 3-week lockout, not a 2-day wait.

3 · Add a 50% mid-month warning: “You’re using it faster than usual.”

The recommendation is to use both rhythms where each fits. Pro stays weekly because those users are frequent and heavy, and a short wait keeps them engaged and buying top-ups. Free access is daily: 3 questions a day for the first 3 days, then AI and In House lock until the user subscribes. Top-ups carry past the reset and stay valid for 3 months. If leadership still prefers monthly for Pro, three things must change: top-up sizes, the price of the smallest pack, and an early warning around the middle of the month.
